{{/* The title of one heading in the table of contents. Takes a dict with `page` and `title`, the .Title of a heading from .Fragments. Hugo renders the title to HTML, but leaves math in it as the source text with its delimiters. Each run of inline math is rendered here through layouts/_markup/render-passthrough.html, the same as in the content. The delimiters must match the inline ones in hugo.toml's passthrough config. The title is HTML escaped, so the math is unescaped before rendering. */ -}} {{- $title := .title -}} {{- range findRE `\$[^$]+\$|\\\(.+?\\\)` .title | uniq -}} {{- $title = replace $title . ($.page.RenderString (htmlUnescape .)) -}} {{- end -}} {{- $title | safeHTML -}}
